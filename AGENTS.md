# MASTER SKILL: Frontend Design Directive

Always treat `.agents/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md` (and the `frontend-design` skill) as the mandatory, primary design standard for this project:

1. **Distinctive & Intentional Visual Identity:** Avoid clichéd, generic, or template-like AI defaults (no generic warm-cream/terracotta combinations, no arbitrary monospace labels, no identical rounded SaaS cards with lazy shadows).
2. **Subject-Grounded Design:** Ground all visual choices, palettes, typography, and hierarchy directly in the platform's educational/tech subject matter.
3. **Typography & Layout:** Choose deliberate, characterful typefaces; adhere to intentional weights and scale; avoid accenting single words in headlines or slapping ALL-CAPS on labels.
4. **Restraint & Meaningful Motion:** Spend boldness in one memorable focal point. Never use scattered non-user-triggered animations.
5. **Purposeful Copywriting:** Write from the learner's perspective in clean, active voice with zero filler or stock AI fluff.
